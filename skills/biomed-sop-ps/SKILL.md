# Workflow

Determine essay purpose from the exact prompt. Academic SOP connects preparation, scientific reasoning, training needs, verified program fit and future direction. Personal history essays address context and contributions when requested. Use existing evidence ledger and program brief or build a concise source map. Explain research through question, personal decision, action, evidence, interpretation and learning. Preserve uncertainty and independence level. Avoid CV repetition, invented hardship and childhood motivations. Do not force a doctoral proposal onto a taught master application. Deliver clean English prose, computed word count and counting convention, separate source map and Chinese revision notes. Check prompt coverage, names, length and factual consistency. Unknown length limits remain provisional.

Use actual documents and explicit applicant statements only. Record source locations, uncertainty and conflicts. Read available materials before asking for information already present. Preserve originals and write drafts separately. Keep private documents local and web searches limited to public program/research terms. Default to Chinese discussion and English application prose. Follow exact university prompts and assistance rules. Never invent achievements, clinical experience or results. Use available document tools for requested Word/PDF output and report unreadable pages. Keep private facts out of installed skill files.
