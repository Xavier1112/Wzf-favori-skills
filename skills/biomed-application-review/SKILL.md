# Workflow

Compare drafts with source material and official prompts. Report prioritized findings with passage, evidence, consequence and suggested revision. Reconcile dates, grades, degree names, methods, individual roles, results and publication status. Flag unsupported claims and unverified program details. Check research reasoning, training need, prompt coverage, word limits and repeated anecdotes. Preserve different referee perspectives. Do not invent admissions probabilities. Revise when requested and list unresolved questions separately.

Use actual documents and explicit applicant statements only. Record source locations, uncertainty and conflicts. Read available materials before asking for information already present. Preserve originals and write drafts separately. Keep private documents local and web searches limited to public program/research terms. Default to Chinese discussion and English application prose. Follow exact university prompts and assistance rules. Never invent achievements, clinical experience or results. Use available document tools for requested Word/PDF output and report unreadable pages. Keep private facts out of installed skill files.
