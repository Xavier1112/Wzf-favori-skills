# Workflow

Establish referee role, relationship dates and firsthand observations from source materials. Label drafts for referee review and adoption; never imply approval. Self-reported achievements cannot become firsthand observations without confirmation. Support evaluation with specific observed behavior. Differentiate letters by research, classroom or project evidence. Never invent rankings, comparison groups, quotes, titles, contact details or signatures. If evidence is inadequate prepare a briefing pack with focused questions. Deliver draft plus separate verification notes. Check consistency with CV and essays. Do not send letters.

Use actual documents and explicit applicant statements only. Record source locations, uncertainty and conflicts. Read available materials before asking for information already present. Preserve originals and write drafts separately. Keep private documents local and web searches limited to public program/research terms. Default to Chinese discussion and English application prose. Follow exact university prompts and assistance rules. Never invent achievements, clinical experience or results. Use available document tools for requested Word/PDF output and report unreadable pages. Keep private facts out of installed skill files.
